Brand Philosophy

About AROMA HOUSE

Dedicated to pure fragrance elevations, disciplined presentation, and absolute pricing integrity.

AROMA HOUSE was established to offer fragrance connoisseurs a sanctuary of olfactory authenticity. Rather than overcomplicating with hundreds of diluted lines, we focus our craft on five distinctive creations comprising the Elevation Series.

Our catalog highlights five signature directions: White Oud Elevation, Oud Supreme Elevation, Ghilaf-E-Kaba Elevation, Oud Ispahan Elevation, and Silver Oud Elevation V-2025. Each formula is measured in extract concentration to preserve the natural warmth, rich sillage, and enduring presence of pure perfume oils.

"We believe true fragrance appreciation begins with honesty: authentic concentration, meticulous measures, and crystal-clear pricing."

Every fragrance is standardized across 3 ml, 6 ml, and 12 ml volumes, accompanied by fixed published retail pricing in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).
