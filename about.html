About AROMA HOUSE

Dedicated to curating fine fragrance compositions with clarity and aesthetic restraint.

AROMA HOUSE was established to bring curated fragrance expressions to connoisseurs who value authenticity, disciplined presentation, and honest value.

Our principal offering, the Elevation Collection, reflects an intentional focus on distinct fragrance profiles. Comprising White Oud Elevation, Oud Supreme Elevation, Ghilaf-E-Kaba Elevation, Oud Ispahan Elevation, and Silver Oud Elevation V-2025, each composition is formulated for personal distinction.

We adhere to a standard of absolute price transparency. All creations are made available across uniform volume tiers—3 ml, 6 ml, and 12 ml—allowing patrons to select the volume that best complements their personal ritual.
